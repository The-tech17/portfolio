# FlyRank Internship & AI Projects (EcoTrace & ArenaGenius2026)

## The Problem

During the FlyRank.ai AI Internship (July 2026 – Present), the objective was to solve real-world automation, data logging, and information summarization problems using low-code/vibe-coding workflows and prompt engineering:

1. **EcoTrace**: Personal environmental tracking is hampered by complex carbon calculation formulas that deter everyday users from logging daily transport and energy consumption.
2. **ArenaGenius2026**: Sports fans face fragmented, clickbait-heavy news feeds when attempting to track schedule updates for major global tournaments like the FIFA World Cup 2026.

---

## What I Built

1. **EcoTrace**: An Android application combining Android Studio and Google Gemini API that calculates carbon footprint metrics from natural language user inputs (e.g., "Drove 15km in an SUV") and logs historical environmental impact.
2. **ArenaGenius2026**: A Python/Streamlit sports intelligence hub that aggregates verified tournament schedules and provides objective, AI-generated news summaries and trivia responses.

---

## My Decisions

- **Natural Language Input for Metrics**: Replaced complex manual dropdown forms in EcoTrace with natural language processing powered by Gemini prompts.
- **Aggregated Summarization**: Built verified source scraping and prompt summarization in ArenaGenius2026 to filter out sensationalist clickbait.

---

## How AI Helped

- **Structured Output Extraction**: Used Gemini API to parse natural language activity logs directly into quantitative metric JSON objects (`{ "category": "transport", "co2_kg": 2.8 }`).
- **Prompt Chaining**: Connected search fetching with summarization prompts to produce reliable news briefs.
