**Target Duration**: 4 Minutes  
**Audience**: FlyRank Reviewers, Hiring Managers, Technical Peers  

---

### Minute 0:00 – 0:45 | Introduction & One-Line Claim

> "Hello! I'm Sanjana Londhe, a B.Tech candidate in Computer Science & Business Systems at Medi-Caps University and an AI Intern at FlyRank.ai.
> 
> My core focus is **Prompt Engineering, Vibe Coding, and AI Application Development**.

---

### Minute 0:45 – 2:00 | Featured Project Walkthrough (BUDS & Visa Helper)

> "Let's look at **BUDS**, my Kotlin Android application. BUDS is an empathetic conversational buddy built for users who need creative or emotional space without having their private messages stored forever. I integrated Google Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT APIs alongside native Text-to-Speech, giving users multiple AI personalities and privacy by design.
> 
> Next is **Visa Helper AI**, built with Python and Streamlit. Applying for visas often involves wading through 50-page policy manuals. I built a RAG pipeline that converts official immigration guidelines into clean, dynamic document checklists with a calm pastel UI toggle."

---

### Minute 2:00 – 3:00 | Technical Architecture & Dynamic Features

> "For the portfolio architecture itself, I chose Option 2: a modular Single-Page Application powered by vanilla JavaScript, clean CSS, and a dynamic JSON data renderer. 
> 
> Notice how fast the site loads—under 1 second with zero build overhead. Pressing `Ctrl+K` opens a global command palette allowing instant search across projects, skills, and case study modals."

---

### Minute 3:00 – 4:00 | Honest Reflections, Win, Limitation & Conclusion

> "One **real win** during this project was engineering prompts that reliably extract quantitative JSON data from unstructured natural language inputs in EcoTrace.
> 
> One **real limitation** is that BUDS currently requires manual API key configuration when compiled outside of default environment builds.
> 
> Thank you for your time! I invite you to explore the active live builds and source repositories linked throughout my portfolio."
