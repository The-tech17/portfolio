# Visual Identity Kit

## Brand Foundations

A restrained, clean, technical dark-mode design system optimized for high readability, subtle glassmorphism, and work-first content focus.

---

## Typography

- **Heading Font**: Inter (`font-sans`, bold weights 600/700)
- **Body Font**: Inter (`font-sans`, regular weights 400/500, line-height 1.6)
- **Code / Technical Font**: Monospace (`font-mono`, for stack badges, timelines, and status tags)

---

## Logo & Favicon Specification

- **Text Mark**: `Sanjana's Studio` set in Inter Bold with clean letter-spacing.
- **Favicon**: Minimalist dark square icon with crisp white initials `SL`.
