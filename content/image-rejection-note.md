# Image Policy & Rejection Note

## Strict Policy Against Generic AI & Stock Images

> [!CAUTION]
> **Zero Tolerance for Fake Artifacts**: The portfolio strictly forbids using synthetic 3D AI renders, fake robotic headshots, stock photography, or decorative futuristic banners.

---

## Rejection Rules

1. **Reject Generic Stock Photos**: Do not use generic images of laptops, coffee cups, or abstract code glowing in neon blues.
2. **Reject Synthetic AI Avatars**: The profile image must be a real photograph of Sanjana Londhe.
3. **Reject Mock UI Frames**: Do not overlay screenshots inside fake isometric 3D device mockups that obscure the real app UI.
4. **Placeholder Protocol**: If an actual screenshot of an Android APK or live app is currently missing, insert an explicit text card with exact capture instructions: `[TO ADD: Screenshot of Buds Android APK conversation screen]`.
