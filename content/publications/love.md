# Love, and only love

## Introduction
*Love, and only love* is a collection of short prose, introspective poetry, and reflective essays. It explores the landscapes of human intimacy, the friction of digital interactions, and the silent vulnerabilities we carry in modern society.

## Excerpt: "Silence in the Digital Age"
> "We speak in pixels and measure affection in blue ticks. Yet, the spaces between our messages are where the true weight of our words reside. We have mastered the art of immediate connection, but are slowly forgetting the beauty of lingering silences."

## Key Themes
*   **Intimacy**: Navigating proximity and distance in human relationships.
*   **Vulnerability**: The courage to be seen in a world obsessed with curated perfection.
*   **Friction**: The quiet tension between physical presence and virtual representations.
